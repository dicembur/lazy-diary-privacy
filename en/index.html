Lazy Diary Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Lazy Diary (the "App") does not collect your personal information or transmit it outside your device. This document explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Personal information we collect

The App does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

2. Entries you write

  • The diary entries you write, along with their dates and times, are stored only on your device.
  • App settings, such as the display language, the names and colors of categories (palettes), and whether categories are turned on, are also stored only on your device.
  • The App reads your device's language setting to choose its display language. This information is not sent anywhere.
  • Your entries are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party, and the developer cannot access them.
  • The App works without an internet connection.

3. Analytics and advertising

The App contains no usage analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

4. Backup files

  • A backup file (JSON) is created only when you choose "Export backup file" in Settings.
  • A backup file contains your entries and the names and colors of your categories. Entries in the trash are not included.
  • You decide where to save a backup file and with whom to share it, and you are responsible for keeping it safe.
  • When you restore, the App reads only the file you select, and that file is not sent anywhere.

5. Device backups

Depending on your device settings, the App's data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. This is a feature provided by your operating system (Apple); the App does not send this data itself.

6. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the App also deletes the entries stored on your device.
  • When you delete an entry in the App, it first moves to the trash and is automatically and permanently deleted from your device after 7 days. If you empty the trash in Settings, entries are permanently deleted immediately.
  • Backup files you have already exported must be deleted by you.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about privacy, please email dicember@gmail.com.
